## 🔒 Access and privacy

> [!IMPORTANT]
> `agent-hub` and `docs` are **private**. They must be reachable only by authorized, named people. Never make them public, share them by "anyone with the link", or copy their content into public places.

<details>
<summary><b>How do I get access?</b></summary>

<br>

1. Make sure you have a GitHub account.
2. Ask the organization owner, Mohammad Ariful ISLAM, to add you to the repository you need.
3. Accept the invitation from GitHub.
4. Open the repository link. If you still see "404 Not Found", you are not yet authorized.

Grant the lowest level of access that lets a person do their job, and review who has access regularly.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What must never be committed to any repository?</b></summary>

<br>

| ❌ Never | ✅ Instead |
|---|---|
| Passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys | An approved secret store |
| Patient or beneficiary records, health or safeguarding case data | The approved program system |
| Confidential exports or database dumps | Restricted, access-controlled storage |
| Personal data in names, issues, or logs | Anonymized or aggregate data |

</details>

## ⏳ How we work

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

**🌿 One permanent branch**<br>
`main` is the only permanent branch. Changes arrive through reviewed pull requests, and temporary branches are deleted after merge.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

**✅ Checks on every push**<br>
Automatic validation runs on each push and pull request, so problems are caught before they reach `main`.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

**🧑‍⚖️ Human in the loop**<br>
People approve consequential actions: publishing, sharing, deleting, and anything that touches real data.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## 📌 About

**Community Partners International (CPI)** works to strengthen, equip and connect local organizations in Myanmar, Bangladesh and Thailand providing health and humanitarian services to conflict- and poverty-affected communities. 

To Learn more, visit our official website at [cpintl.org](https://cpintl.org/) 

**Community Partners International (CPI) - 🇧🇩 Bangladesh Mission**, partners with Local NGOs, CSOs, provides funding, technical expertise and contributes in the sustainable development of Bangladesh, since 2017. 

To Learn more, visit our official website at [cpintl.org/bangladesh](https://cpintl.org/bangladesh/).
